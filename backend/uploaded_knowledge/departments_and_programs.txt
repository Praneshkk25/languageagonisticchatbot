# Sona College of Technology - Official Academic Departments and Programs Offered

## 1. Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- **Programs Offered**:
  - **Under Graduate (UG)**:
    - B.E. Computer Science and Engineering (Duration: 4 Years)
    - B.E. CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) (Duration: 4 Years)
    - B.E. Computer Science and Design (Duration: 4 Years)
    - B.E. Computer Science and Business System (Duration: 4 Years)
    - B.E. Computer Science and Engineering (Cyber Security) (Duration: 4 Years)
  - **Post Graduate (PG)**:
    - M.E. Computer Science and Engineering (Duration: 2 Years)
  - **Doctoral Program**:
    - Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering (Full-Time & Part-Time)
- **Eligibility & Admission**: Higher Secondary (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics through Single Window Counselling (TNEA / Anna University) or Consortium / Management Entrance Exam.
